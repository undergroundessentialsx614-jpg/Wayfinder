WAYFINDER PROTOTYPE

This is a simple installable-web-app starter project, not a native App Store app.

TO TRY IT:
1. Upload the contents of this folder to a static HTTPS host (for example, a website hosting service).
2. Open the hosted website in Safari on your iPhone.
3. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
4. Launch WayFinder from your Home Screen.

IMPORTANT:
- The included hospital/school directions are sample text only.
- The floor plan is illustrative and not to scale.
- Real navigation requires permission to use a building's current floor plans, verified room data, accessible routes, and indoor positioning or a manually selected starting point.
- This prototype does not locate the phone indoors or provide emergency guidance.
